## What exists
- **MN: numerical only (as found).**
  - Gair, Li & Mandel, PRD 77 024035 (2008), arXiv:0708.0628 (read, §II, for Tier 2a): ergodic and chaotic orbits
    found numerically.
  - Apostolatos, Lukes-Gerakopoulos & Contopoulos, PRL 103 111101 (2009): Birkhoff plateaus, numerical.
  - Contopoulos, Lukes-Gerakopoulos & Apostolatos, IJBC 21 2261 (2011), arXiv:1108.5057: surfaces of section, chaotic
    orbits in the inner region.
  - Lukes-Gerakopoulos et al., arXiv:1408.4697: rotation numbers, numerical.
  - The non-integrability argument in this literature is "no Carter-like constant", not a proof.
- **Rigorous (analytic, perturbative) chaos for perturbed black-hole geodesics:**
  - Moeckel, "A nonintegrable model in general relativity", CMP 150 (1992) 415–430, DOI 10.1007/BF02096667: perturbed
    Schwarzschild (a relativistic Hill problem), with a chaotic invariant set. It cites Robinson's Melnikov work.
  - Bombelli & Calzetta, "Chaos around a black hole", CQG 9 (1992) 2573–2599, DOI 10.1088/0264-9381/9/12/004: Melnikov,
    for Schwarzschild under periodic perturbations.
  - Letelier & Vieira, CQG 14 (1997), DOI 10.1088/0264-9381/14/5/026, arXiv:gr-qc/9706025: Melnikov, for Schwarzschild plus gravitational waves.
  - Polcar & Semerák, PRD 100, 103013 (2019), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.100.103013, arXiv:1911.09790: Melnikov, for
    Schwarzschild plus a disc or ring.
    ("Suggests"/"fits" in the abstract wording; not checked for rigour.)
- **Computer-assisted homoclinic chaos, non-relativistic:** e.g. Murray & Mireles James, arXiv:2212.00930 (the
  restricted four-body problem), and Capiński–Kepley–Mireles James, arXiv:2205.03922 (the restricted three-body problem;
  the source of our validator's positive control).
- **Our own earlier work on MN:** Morales–Ramis/Ziglin non-integrability obstructions (meromorphic, axis and equator),
  and a certified first-order Melnikov coefficient. Neither gives h_top > 0 on its own.

## Safe wording
- **OK:** "To our knowledge, the first computer-assisted proof of chaos (a transversal homoclinic orbit, hence positive
  topological entropy) for geodesics of the Manko–Novikov spacetime. Earlier MN chaos results we found are numerical."
- **OK:** "a rigorous small-β result (Melnikov type) for MN, adding to the perturbative results known for perturbed
  Schwarzschild (Moeckel 1992; Bombelli & Calzetta 1992; Letelier & Vieira 1997)."
- **NOT OK:**
  - "the first rigorous proof of chaos around a black hole": Moeckel and Bombelli–Calzetta precede us;
  - "first" without "to our knowledge";
  - any claim about other levels.
- **Open:** a check by a domain expert; a citation search on Moeckel 1992 for later rigorous work on Kerr-type
  deformations.
